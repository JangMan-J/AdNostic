# AdNostic privacy policy

AdNostic collects no data. It does not store, transmit or share personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics and no server, and it makes no network requests of its own.

**What it does.** It runs locally in your browser. It examines the requests and scripts of the pages you visit in order to block intrusive ads and pop-unders, block ads containing explicit content, and disable scripts that try to override your ad-blocking settings. None of this is recorded or sent anywhere.

**What it stores.** Two settings, in the browser's local extension storage: the global on/off switch, and the list of site names you chose to pause AdNostic on. They stay on your device and websites cannot read them. The per-tab counters shown on the toolbar badge are kept in memory only and discarded when the tab closes.

**What it changes on pages.** It blocks requests to a bundled list of ad hosts, hides ad and overlay elements, and answers some page checks. On a few specific sites it also sets one small cookie so that the site stops showing a pop-under or an ad-blocker prompt. That cookie is created in your browser and is not read or sent by AdNostic.

**Permissions.** Access to all sites is needed because intrusive ads and the scripts that circumvent ad blocking can appear on any site. It is used only on the page you are viewing, only on your device.

**Changes.** Any change to this policy will be published at this address.
